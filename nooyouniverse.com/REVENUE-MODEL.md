## 1. Authority and reading rules

This document converts the portfolio's six-year planning brief into a Noo-specific decision package. It does not approve pricing, a checkout, a payment processor, a subscription, an app, a campaign, a provider account, outreach, spending, health-data processing, or publication.

Use these state words literally:

- **Verified:** supported by the cited repository or portfolio record as of its stated date.
- **Proposed:** ready for owner review; not approved or operational.
- **Approved:** requires an explicit owner decision for the exact scope.
- **Specialist-cleared:** requires the named qualified review; owner approval alone is insufficient.
- **Live / sent / sold / earned:** use only after the relevant external event and readback or financial evidence exist.

Planning-goal figures below are scenario controls, not forecasts, valuations, commitments, or evidence of demand. Gross sales, recognized revenue, cash collected, and contribution margin are different measures. No planning goal may be copied into public marketing as an expected result.

## 3. Portfolio role

### Recommended role

Treat Noo YouNiverse as an **audience-and-trust brand**, not one of the portfolio's primary venture-scale infrastructure engines.

Noo's job is to:

1. make evidence literacy memorable through a clearly fictional, AI-assisted host;
2. prove that a persona-led publication can earn repeat attention without fabricated authority;
3. build an owned, consented audience;
4. create reusable educational intellectual property and process evidence;
5. provide an honest showcase for MyPersonas without pretending that Noo itself owns platform-scale infrastructure; and
6. test whether a bounded education business exists before app, B2B, sponsorship, affiliate, or physical-product expansion.

### Portfolio boundary

MyPersonas + AliaSpaces, with Soul Concept Engine as proposed rails, is the portfolio's venture-scale platform candidate. Noo may become a customer, showcase, or licensed-content supplier to that platform, but cross-brand identity, data, economics, and intellectual-property terms must be decided rather than silently blended.

Maintain a Noo-level profit-and-loss view for learning. Do not infer that every Noo audience member, subscriber, or licensed artifact is automatically a MyPersonas customer, or vice versa.

## 9. Commercialization conflicts and required firewalls

### Evidence versus commerce

- A graded claim card, source table, correction, or safety limitation must never disappear behind a paywall.
- A sponsor, affiliate, customer, product team, or sales target cannot set an evidence tier, suppress a null result, narrow a correction, or control source selection.
- Never analyze a sponsor or owned product inside its paid placement.
- No commerce CTA adjacent to a claim whose review could influence that transaction until the exact conflict model is independently approved.
- Maintain a public conflict disclosure and an internal append-only decision/correction record for affected artifacts.

### Fictional host versus authority

- Cillian is a fictional, AI-assisted teaching interface—not a clinician, scientist, customer, or product tester.
- Paid products cannot convert character voice into implied testimonial, professional credential, or human experience.
- Every offer must still make sense in plain English with the fictional wrapper removed.

### Education versus medical function

- Mission packs, courses, memberships, and B2B licenses teach claim inspection; they do not diagnose, treat, prescribe, personalize, or promise health outcomes.
- App pricing cannot make a safety control, crisis resource, correction, export needed for care, or core risk boundary contingent on payment.
- A wellness/EAP buyer does not convert Noo content into clinical content; contract and presentation must preserve the educational boundary.

### Privacy versus growth

- Newsletter clicks must not become inferred health-interest segments.
- No advertising pixel, behavioral-ad SDK, or full-session replay belongs on health surfaces.
- Private logs, risk tiers, FHIR data, conditions, substances, and community reports cannot feed acquisition, upsell, pricing, churn prevention, or sales targeting.
- Cross-brand data sharing requires a separate owner decision, user-facing explanation, lawful basis, and review; portfolio ownership is not consent.

### Community versus monetization

- Community browse/submission remains closed until all launch gates; commerce provides no reason to weaken eligibility, privacy, suppression, moderation, or `k ≥ 20` controls.
- No brand or own-SKU reports, shopping links, positive-outcome ranking, incentives, or use of community reports in Noo marketing under the current recommended boundary.
- Community access based on a sensitive profile is never itself a paid benefit.

### Physical products versus editorial independence

Physical products are a separate later business case. If considered, separate personnel, decision records, budgets, claims review, correction authority, and success metrics from evidence editorial work. The evidence library must be able to conclude **Unsupported**, **Mixed**, or **Unknown** about any commercially relevant claim without business interference.

## 10. Experiment ladder

The ladder is sequential. Passing one rung permits an owner decision on the next; it does not automatically launch it.

| Rung | Proposed experiment | Evidence to collect | Advance condition | Stop / revise condition |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Operational baseline | Account roles/status, four weeks of comparable content, production/review time, corrections, source clicks, return behavior, owned-channel joins | Baseline is trustworthy and the workflow is sustainable | Missing permissions, missing disclosure/review, review debt, or unsafe-response load pauses distribution |
| 1 | Trust-format validation | 8–12 approved methodology artifacts; repeat viewing, substantive comments, source use, identity confusion, unsafe questions | At least one repeatable format produces qualified return or source behavior without safety/identity degradation | No repeat signal after the test set → revise hook/format; persistent fictional/clinical confusion → simplify or remove the wrapper |
| 2 | Owned-audience validation | Properly confirmed subscribers, consent source, unsubscribe/complaint/bounce, return visits, production cost | Readers voluntarily confirm and return; operations remain compliant and supportable | No double opt-in infrastructure, unclear consent, sender/DNS defects, or unsafe segmentation → no send |
| 3 | Non-transactional offer discovery | One exact concept page or owner-approved interview script; qualified problem, desired format, objections, support expectations | Owner-approved minimum demand threshold is met without invented urgency or health promises | Weak demand, demand centered on prohibited personalization, or unclear deliverable → revise/kill before pricing |
| 4 | Capped paid education pilot | Orders, refunds, completion/use, support/review hours, source/correction events, contribution margin, repeat intent | Positive unit contribution and acceptable review/support load across a pre-approved cap | Any unsupported claim, fulfillment failure, excessive refund/support burden, or negative unit contribution → stop sales and correct/refund as required |
| 5 | Recurring membership test | Activation, retained paid cohorts, content/review capacity, voluntary churn reasons, contribution margin | Retention is driven by educational value, not hidden safety/source access | Churn, content treadmill, review debt, or paywall conflict → return to one-off education |
| 6 | B2B discovery and pilot | Only after outreach approval: buyer problem, legal/procurement cycle, pilot scope, update/correction burden, renewal intent | At least two qualified buyers from ten authorized discovery conversations request a scoped paid pilot; this is a proposed default threshold for owner approval | Buyer requires clinical claims, data access, exclusivity over corrections, or sponsor control → refuse; no pilot pull → pause B2B |
| 7 | Private-log subscription | Synthetic prototype evidence, real-data gates, security/privacy/clinical review, support/incident readiness, safe free/paid boundary | Product utility and willingness to pay exist without recommendations or monetizing sensitive data | Safety features would be paywalled, duty-of-care cost is unfunded, or privacy/security gates fail → do not accept real data |
| 8 | Sponsorship/affiliate pilot | Conflict review, disclosure comprehension, advertiser influence requests, audience trust signals | Independent review confirms no effect on evidence or corrections | Any pressure on claims, ranking, source selection, or correction timing → terminate relationship |

The only numerical threshold proposed here is the B2B discovery default. All other conversion, retention, refund, margin, and capacity thresholds must be set after Rung 0 produces a denominator and before the relevant experiment begins.

## 11. Kill, hold, and pivot criteria

### Immediate pause conditions

- Any Cillian artifact states or implies lived product use, clinical credentials, or individualized advice.
- A paid or sponsored relationship influences a source, evidence label, limitation, null result, or correction.
- A public offer or product makes an unreviewed health, efficacy, privacy, security, anonymity, or launch claim.
- Newsletter or product data crosses into health targeting, advertising, or cross-brand use without the separate reviewed authorization.
- A safety resource, correction, source limitation, or essential export becomes paywalled.
- Payment, refund, tax, delivery, support, consent, deletion, or incident handling cannot operate as promised.

### Engine-level hold or pivot rules

| Engine | Hold / kill signal | Preferred pivot |
|---|---|---|
| Mission packs / source tools | Buyers primarily seek personalized product advice; source/rights review costs exceed viable contribution; repeat use is absent | Free methodology library, smaller general evidence-literacy tool, or stop |
| Membership | Retention requires excessive cadence, hides public trust material, or creates review debt | One-off packs, seasonal cohorts, or free newsletter |
| Courses | Demand depends on credential/clinical-outcome implication; completion/support burden is unsustainable | Short workshops or licensed general media-literacy modules |
| Private-log app | Safety/privacy infrastructure and incident duty cannot be funded; safety would be paywalled; Track A boundary cannot hold | Device-local/synthetic research only, or stop the product while continuing media |
| B2B licensing | Buyers require health personalization, employee-health data, exclusivity over correction, or implied endorsement | License only general claim-inspection training, or stop B2B |
| Sponsorship/affiliate | Trust declines, advertiser influence appears, or conflict cannot be explained plainly | Reader-supported education with no sponsor adjacency |
| Physical products | Editorial evidence work begins serving SKU sales; qualification/recall/adverse-event operations are incomplete | Keep commerce on Hold; use independent third-party education only |
| Fictional-host format | Persistent identity confusion survives clearer disclosure and testing | Move Cillian to an occasional narrator and lead in plain-language institutional voice |

### Portfolio pivot rule

If Noo produces trust and educational IP but not a supportable standalone business, treat it as a portfolio audience/IP property feeding the MyPersonas showcase rather than forcing an independent revenue target. That outcome is strategically usable and more honest than adding unsafe engines to make the spreadsheet grow.
